Design study: the data platform

Publish once. Keep it current. Serve it.

The data platform is one service of the platform, built around a Data Spine: streaming and event-driven at its core, a Streamhouse for current state, and an analytical plane kept in sync. It is a design study and does not run in my lab yet.

  1. Publish once.One backbone would turn producers times consumers into producers plus consumers, for transport. Meanings and contracts still differ.
  2. Shift left.Silver is built once, in the stream, and owned by its domain.
  3. Shift left, again.The Streamhouse, an open pattern, builds the application gold layer where the data sits.
  4. Event-driven where it is needed.An application that must react subscribes to the streams. One that only needs current state reads a live view over SQL or REST.
  5. Kept in sync.Streams would land in the analytical plane as tables, under a stated contract for lag, reconciliation and backfill.
