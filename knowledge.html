Deep dive: the knowledge service

One service, many domains

The knowledge service holds what is known, in domains with a scope and an owner. Governance is the one domain that binds.

  1. Service and content.One service stores and serves. The domains are what it holds.
  2. Domain and scope.What it is about, and who it is for.
  3. Only one domain binds.Governance is knowledge that has been ratified.
  4. Grounded, not remembered.A session asks the service before it reasons, and again after its context is compacted.
