Deep dive: the Sinapsi Dev Factory

From a goal to a merged change

People build through the Factory: a person sets the goal, and agent sessions design, build, review and operate under the rules.

  1. Three seams.Into governance it reads and proposes. Into the platform it operates through the corridor. Into applications it designs and builds.
  2. The orchestrator governs.It splits, briefs and verifies. It does not absorb the work.
  3. Separation of duties.The session that builds a change never merges it.
  4. A person is asked rarely.Once, bundled, and only when a step cannot go on.
